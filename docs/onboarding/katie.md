# Welcome, Katie

*Treasurer, ZeroPoint Foundation.*

The ZeroPoint Foundation is a Wyoming nonprofit. Its purpose is to build and
maintain a trust substrate for autonomous systems — software that authorizes
its own actions cryptographically and keeps a tamper-evident audit chain of
everything it does. Your role as treasurer puts you in the foundation's
governance, with read access across the workspace and the authority to act on
foundation finances.

## What "treasurer" means here, operationally

You can:

- **Read every mailbox** the foundation hosts, so you can see correspondence
  that touches finances regardless of who originally received it.
- **Send and manage your own mailbox** (`katie@zeropointfoundation.org`),
  including replying as `info@` for shared correspondence.
- **Read and write documents** — financial reports, board minutes, foundation
  records.
- **Read and write tasks** — track financial commitments, deadlines, filings.
- **Read receipts** — see the audit chain, which records every authorized
  action by every operator.

Each of these capabilities is checked by the system on every action. You
can't accidentally do something outside your grant.

## Logging in

Once Ken has put your real public key in the system, you'll go to
`zeropointfoundation.org/mail/` and request a session. Your name and role
will appear in the top corner of the workspace. If anything looks wrong —
especially if it shows a role you don't recognize — stop and message Ken
directly.

## Recovery — read this once carefully

When your keypair is generated (Ken or the system will walk you through it),
you'll be shown a 24-word recovery phrase. **Write it down on paper. Don't
photograph it. Don't email it. Don't store it in a cloud notes app.** Put the
paper somewhere you'd put an important physical document. If your computer is
ever lost, stolen, or replaced, those 24 words are how you get back in.

If anyone — including Ken, including someone who says they're from the
foundation — ever asks you to share or type your recovery phrase outside of a
recovery flow you initiated, the answer is no.

## What to expect during onboarding

Ken will walk you through the seven steps in `STAFF-ONBOARDING.md`. The whole
thing should take an hour the first time. The two parts that matter most:

1. **Generating your keypair** — your computer creates the keys, the private
   one never leaves your device.
2. **The capability self-test** — you'll deliberately attempt one action
   that's allowed and one that isn't. The denial is logged in the audit
   chain. That denial is the proof your boundaries work.

## Questions

Ken is the right person for anything system-related. For role-specific

form suits you.

Welcome aboard.
