Provable governance

ZeroPoint gives you cryptographically verifiable accountability — signed receipts, narrowing capability grants, and non-removable constitutional rules — without a central authority.

The Problem

The accountability gap is structural.

AI agents act across organizational boundaries at machine speed, yet the systems beneath them still rely on mutable logs, informal permissions, and platform-controlled identity. When something goes wrong, there is no shared, independently verifiable record of what happened or who authorized it.

The Solution

Protocol-level accountability.

ZeroPoint makes every action provable using three cryptographic primitives: hash-chained receipts, portable capability grants that strictly narrow with each delegation, and two constitutional rules that evaluate before every action and cannot be removed. These guarantees work identically across any transport.

Core Primitives

Three primitives. One substrate.

Receipts

Every action produces a signed, hash-chained receipt. The pr field references the predecessor, making the chain tamper-evident: modifying any entry requires rewriting every subsequent one and convincing every independent verifier to accept the rewrite. Chains verify offline, cold, with no running server.

Ed25519 Blake3 150–300 bytes MessagePack

Capability Grants

Signed authorization tokens specifying scope, time window, cost ceiling, delegation depth, and minimum trust tier. Any grant holder can delegate a subset. Eight invariants enforce that authority narrows at every step — never widens. A revoked parent invalidates all descendants.

8 delegation invariants scope narrowing offline-verifiable depth-limited

Constitutional Rules

HarmPrincipleRule and SovereigntyRule evaluate before every action at every node. Fixed at positions 1 and 2 in the PolicyEngine — they cannot be removed, reordered, or overridden by any capability grant, WASM module, or operator action.

non-removable locally enforced no coordinator WASM cannot override
For Builders

Built for integration.

ZeroPoint is written in Rust and designed to be embedded into agents, services, and infrastructure. It ships with a Reticulum-compatible mesh transport, TCP/UDP interfaces, an HTTP API, and a dual-backend peer discovery layer called the Presence Plane.
