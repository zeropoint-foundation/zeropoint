Read these together or not at all. A tie-off is an honest governance acknowledgement that something is known-unbuilt — and it moves the headline without changing what the substrate does. Tie-offs grew 10.6×; live connections grew 2.05×.

And they are not one mechanism. Of 433 tie-offs, 1 carries a disposition, a declared date and a reopen condition (tieoffs.toml, whose own header insists it is not an allowlist). 60 carry a reason at the site. 372 carry a reason in a source comment — real and often careful, but prose a reader can find and a tool cannot check. All three suppress a defect and all three count toward maturity identically.
